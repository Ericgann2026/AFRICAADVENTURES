How is my booking total calculated?

Your total is based on the selected adventure and number of travelers. The total is shown on the payment page before you complete checkout.

Which payment method do you accept?

Online payments are processed securely through PayPal.

Can I book from another country?

Yes. The booking form accepts international phone numbers and can be completed by travelers worldwide.

How do I share special requests?

Use the Special Requests field in the booking form to share details such as preferences or questions about your trip.

Can I contact you before booking?

Yes. Email us at Africancountriesadventures@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.
