The Big Five

Africa is famous for its incredible wildlife. The Big Five are the lion, elephant, leopard, rhinoceros and buffalo.

🦁 Lion

Known as the king of the African savanna, lions live in prides and are powerful predators.

Where to see them

🇰🇪 Kenya — Masai Mara
🇹🇿 Tanzania — Serengeti
🇿🇦 South Africa — Kruger

🐘 Elephant

African elephants are the world's largest land animals and are known for their intelligence and strong family groups.

Where to see them

🇰🇪 Kenya — Amboseli
🇹🇿 Tanzania — Serengeti
🇿🇦 South Africa — Kruger

🐆 Leopard

Leopards are powerful and secretive big cats. They are excellent climbers and often rest in trees.

Where to see them

🇰🇪 Kenya — Masai Mara
🇹🇿 Tanzania — Serengeti
🇿🇦 South Africa — Kruger

🦏 Rhinoceros

Rhinos are powerful herbivores that play an important role in African ecosystems.

Where to see them

🇰🇪 Kenya — Ol Pejeta
🇹🇿 Tanzania — Ngorongoro
🇿🇦 South Africa — Kruger

🐃 African Buffalo

African buffalo are powerful herd animals and one of the famous Big Five.

Where to see them

🇰🇪 Kenya — Masai Mara
🇹🇿 Tanzania — Serengeti
🇿🇦 South Africa — Kruger

🐆 Cheetah

The cheetah is the fastest land animal and is built for incredible bursts of speed.

Where to see them

🇰🇪 Kenya — Masai Mara
🇹🇿 Tanzania — Serengeti
🇿🇦 South Africa — Kruger
